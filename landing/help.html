Help Center

Answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Email us and a real person will reply.

Need a hand? Send us feedback (30 seconds) or email support@momease.app from the address on your account. We reply within 2 business days.

Signing in

I can't sign in with Google

Make sure you choose the same Google account each time. If you see a blank page afterwards, reload the page once or twice, or clear site data for app.mease.mom in your browser settings, then try again.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email and follow the link we send you. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

My account says the email is already in use

Sign in instead of creating a new account. If you signed up with Google, use Continue with Google.

Subscription

How do I subscribe or manage my plan?

Subscriptions are handled on the web at app.mease.mom. Sign in, open Upgrade and start your 7-day free trial. Your Premium access then appears in the app automatically.

How do I cancel?

See how to cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

I paid but Premium isn't showing

It can take a minute after checkout. Reload the page or reopen the app. If it still isn't showing after 10 minutes, email us with the email address on your account.

Your data

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account in the app, or follow the steps on the delete account page.

What do you do with my information?

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Using the app

Is MomEase only for moms?

No. When you set up your profile choose Not a mom (yet) and MomEase focuses on calm, sleep, stress and self-care without any parenting content. You can change this any time in your profile.

Is MomEase a medical service?

No. MomEase is a wellness companion and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis or need urgent help, contact your local emergency number.
