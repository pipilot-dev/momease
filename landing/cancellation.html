Cancellation & Refund Policy

How to cancel your MomEase subscription and when refunds apply.

Effective August 24, 2026

Cancelling your subscription

  • In the MomEase app — Account → Subscription → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep access until then.
  • Via Google Play — open the Play Store app → menu → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → MomEase → Cancel.
  • Via the App Store — Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MomEase → Cancel Subscription.
  • By email — write to support@momease.app with the email address on your account. We reply within 2 business days.

Refund eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You were charged after cancelling.
  • You were charged an amount different from what was displayed at checkout.
  • A core feature you paid for has been unavailable for more than 7 continuous days through no fault of yours.

You are not eligible for a refund if:

  • You changed your mind after using the app for several days, and no service failure occurred.
  • You cancelled the subscription but forgot before the next renewal — cancellations only affect future billing periods.

Free trial

If we offer a free trial, you will not be charged during the trial period. Cancel any time before the trial ends to avoid charges. If you're charged immediately after a trial and you never opened the app, email us within 7 days and we'll refund it.

How to request a refund

  1. Refunds for App Store subscriptions must be requested via Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Refunds for Google Play subscriptions can be requested via Google Play or by emailing us.
  3. For direct-billed subscriptions (Stripe on the web), email support@momease.app with your order reference.

Timing

  • We respond to every request within 2 business days.
  • Approved refunds post to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.
